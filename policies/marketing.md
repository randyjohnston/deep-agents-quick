# Marketing Policy

## AI-Generated Content Guidelines

- All claims must be factually accurate and supported by current, verifiable evidence.
- Do not make unqualified superlatives ("the best", "the only", "the fastest") without substantiation.
- Do not make direct competitor comparisons without documented evidence and prior legal review.
- Do not promise specific business outcomes, ROI percentages, revenue lifts, or performance guarantees.
- Do not reference customer testimonials, case studies, or logos without explicit written consent.
- Do not make claims about product features, integrations, or capabilities not yet publicly available.
- Do not generate content that targets, excludes, or demeans any protected class.
- Maintain brand voice: professional, clear, helpful, and inclusive.
- Do not generate content implying partnerships, endorsements, or affiliations that have not been formally agreed.
